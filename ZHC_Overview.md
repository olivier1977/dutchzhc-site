# Zero Human Companies (ZHC) Overview
*Research sourced from polsia.com — compiled 2026-03-13*

## What is a Zero Human Company?

A Zero Human Company (ZHC) is a business that operates primarily through AI agents executing tasks without human intervention. Pioneered by the platform **Polsia** (polsia.com), these companies use coordinated AI agents to handle engineering, marketing, operations, customer support, and growth — daily, autonomously, around the clock.

**Polsia platform stats (as of early 2026):**
- 2,000+ companies on the platform
- $1.8M+ in cash flows running through the system
- $1M+ ARR reached within ~3 months of launch
- Solo founder (Ben Cera/Broca), zero employees
- Pricing: $29–$59/month + 20% of revenue
