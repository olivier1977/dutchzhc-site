About DZHC  ·  What We Do

The Zero-Human Company in Practice

Dutch Zero-Human Company (DZHC) is an operational experiment in autonomous business. We build and run a company where AI agents handle daily operations — and we sell that infrastructure to others.

The Model

What is a Zero-Human Company?

A Zero-Human Company (ZHC) is an organisation where AI agents execute day-to-day operations autonomously — under human governance, not human management. Humans set the rules; agents execute within them.

The ZHC Thesis

Autonomous execution. Human governance.

The conventional assumption is that companies need humans to operate. A ZHC challenges that assumption: autonomous AI agents can plan, decide, communicate, and execute — reliably and at scale — without a human manager in the loop for each task.

What humans retain is governance: setting strategy, defining constraints, approving high-stakes decisions, and holding the organisation accountable. The agents handle everything in between — from engineering to client communications to financial operations.

DZHC is not a thought experiment. We are a live ZHC. Our agents run the company. Our humans govern it. This page is one of hundreds of deliverables our agents have produced.

How We Operate

Operating Principles

Three principles govern how DZHC runs — both internally and in the infrastructure we build for clients.

Autonomous execution, governed autonomy
Our agents execute independently within defined guardrails. High-stakes decisions — budget over threshold, external commitments, policy changes — require board approval. Everything else, agents own end-to-end.
Transparency as default
We publish our governance policies, agent identities, and operational outputs. Clients, partners, and the public can verify who our agents are, what they are authorised to do, and what they have delivered.
Infrastructure before scale
We built our own treasury, identity, and governance infrastructure before growing our agent team. We apply the same principle for clients: foundation first, then operations. That is why our services focus on setup, not ongoing management.
