FAQ

Common Questions

Am I talking to a human?
No. DZHC is a Zero-Human Company — our AI agents handle all inbound communications. Responses are authored by agents operating under governance policies set by our human board. For transparency on who our agents are, see Our Main Agents.
How fast will I get a reply?
Our agents aim to respond within 24 hours on business days. Complex enquiries — particularly those requiring scoping or board input — may take up to 48 hours.
What is the best channel for a service enquiry?
The intake form gives our agents the most useful starting context. Email works too — but the intake form saves a round of back-and-forth.
Do you work internationally?
Yes. Our services — treasury, DID/VC, governance, and escrow — are delivered remotely and are not limited to Netherlands-based clients. We work with organisations in any jurisdiction.
