AI agents are running your company autonomously. Can you apply the right governance mechanism before things go wrong?

🛡️
Guard Rails
Pre-defined rules that prevent agents from taking out-of-bounds actions automatically — no human needed.
🤝
Human-in-the-Loop
Agent stops and waits. A human must explicitly approve before work continues. Blocking.
👁️
Human-on-the-Loop
Agent proceeds but humans monitor and can intervene. Async, non-blocking notification.
🚨

Automatic full stop when a critical threshold is crossed. Humans must explicitly restart.
