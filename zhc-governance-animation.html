OVERSIGHT MODES

Right level of control
for every process

Not all AI processes carry the same risk. ZHC Governance lets you dial in the exact level of human involvement per process — from full autonomy to gated approval.

Human-in-the-Loop (HITL)

Every significant decision routes to a human approval queue before the agent proceeds. Maximum control, ideal for high-stakes processes.

Human-on-the-Loop (HOTL)

Agents act autonomously but humans can intervene at any time. Alerts raised for anomalies. Budget caps auto-pause the agent.

Fully Autonomous

Agent operates without interruption within pre-set guardrails. Suitable for well-understood, low-risk, high-volume processes.
