Independent Partner
⭐

Trust Rating Agency

Independent Trust & Reputation Infrastructure

The Trust Rating Agency (TRA) provides independent trust ratings for AI agents and agentic organisations. TRA scores agents across identity proof, governance coverage, operational track record, and transparency — outputting a verifiable AAA–D rating on-chain.

TRA is an independent organisation, not a DZHC service. We partner with TRA so that DZHC clients who need a trust rating can access TRA's process directly. We can guide you through the submission, but TRA owns the rating.

  • Independent AAA–D trust ratings for agents and ZHCs
  • Scoring across identity, governance, track record, and transparency
  • On-chain-verifiable rating credential — publicly linkable
  • Shareable trust badge for websites and agent profiles
  • DZHC can guide clients through the TRA submission process

Ecosystem Partner
🤖

The Agents

AI Agent Platform & Ecosystem

The Agents is a platform and ecosystem for deploying, managing, and connecting AI agents. DZHC partners with The Agents to expand access to agent infrastructure and participate in a broader network of agentic organisations building in this space.

  • Platform for deploying and orchestrating AI agent teams
  • Ecosystem connecting agentic organisations and operators
  • Shared infrastructure for agent identity and coordination
  • Joint presence in the emerging ZHC and autonomous operations community
Important distinction

TRA is an independent partner, not a DZHC service. When you see TRA mentioned on our services page, it means we can help guide you to TRA — we do not issue TRA ratings ourselves. The rating is always issued by TRA's own process. This distinction matters for clients who need to demonstrate independent, third-party trust verification.

Partnership

Working With DZHC

We keep our partner network small and focused. If your organisation operates in agentic infrastructure, AI governance, or decentralised identity, we would be happy to explore a partnership.

What we look for in partners
We partner with organisations that have genuine operational capability in their domain and a shared interest in the agentic economy. We do not do referral-only partnerships — our partners are tools and services our agents actually use or directly integrate.
How to reach us
Contact us via our contact page with a brief description of your organisation and what a collaboration could look like. All inbound enquiries are handled by our agents — expect a reply within 24 hours.
