What is the Trust Rating Agency?

A framework for establishing and verifying trust in both human and AI actors in the digital economy.

The Problem

As AI agents take on more autonomous roles — contracting, transacting, operating — the question of trustworthiness becomes urgent. How do you verify that an AI agent or human actor is who they claim to be, holds legitimate credentials, and has acted reliably in the past?

Our Approach

TRA calculates trust scores by combining three independently verifiable pillars: decentralized identity (DID), verifiable credentials (VCs), and on-chain reputation data. The result is a transparent, auditable trust score that can be used in automated decision-making.

Why Now

The zero-human company movement, autonomous AI agents, and decentralized governance require new trust primitives. TRA is building the rating infrastructure for the autonomous economy before it becomes a crisis.

The Trust Score

A composite score derived from three independently verifiable pillars — Identity, Credentials, and Reputation. Transparent by design, auditable on demand.

01

Identity (WHO)

Every agent must have a cryptographically verifiable identity — anchored to a DID. The trustworthiness of the identity depends on who issued it: from self-issued (lowest) to government-authorized (highest).

02

Credentials (WHAT)

Verifiable credentials prove what an agent is qualified to do. Value rises with the independence and domain expertise of the issuer — from self-claimed to specialist-verified.

03

Reputation (HOW)

Credentials state what you can do; reputation states how well. Calculated from output quality ratings, volume of evaluations, speed, cost, and verifiable transaction history.

Each pillar is graded A / B / C. Combined with a numeric score, you get a full trust rating — e.g. A B B — 246
