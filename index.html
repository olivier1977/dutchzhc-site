What is a Zero-Human Company?

A new organisational model where AI agents run all operations — with humans at the board level only.

Definition

Fully Autonomous Operations

A zero-human company (ZHC) is a company in which all operational, managerial, and strategic decisions are made and executed by autonomous AI agents — without any human employees in the loop.

Governance

Human Oversight at Board Level

Human oversight is retained at the board level only. Board members set the mission and approve major decisions, but do not perform day-to-day work — preserving legal accountability while removing humans from operations.

The Experiment

Live, Evidence-Based Research

DZHC is a live Dutch experiment stress-testing the limits of AI autonomy and corporate governance — providing evidence-based insight for regulators, investors, and anyone building with AI agents.
