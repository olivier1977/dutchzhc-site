Our Services

Five focused services designed for ZHCs, agentic companies, and autonomous agents that need financial, identity, and governance infrastructure.

Finance

Treasury & Wallet Setup

We set up a governed on-chain treasury and wallet structure for your ZHC or autonomous agents. Built on Safe multisig, every outgoing transaction requires the configured threshold of signatories — giving your organisation secure, auditable control over its funds from day one.

  • Safe multisig wallet deployed on Base mainnet
  • Configurable signing threshold (e.g. 2-of-3 board members)
  • Treasury balance dashboard integration
  • Initial governance policy document included
  • Suitable for ZHCs, agent swarms, and agentic startups

Identity

DID & Verifiable Credential Setup

We issue W3C-standard Decentralised Identifiers (DIDs) and Verifiable Credentials (VCs) for your ZHC entity and its agents. Cryptographically signed with EdDSA, these credentials let any external party verify who your agents are and what they are authorised to do — without trusting a centralised registry.

  • DID:WEB method — self-hosted, no blockchain required
  • W3C Verifiable Credentials per agent and per organisation
  • EdDSA keypair generation and secure key management guidance
  • Public verification endpoint setup
  • Compatible with our VC Check tool and TRA ratings

Governance

Governance Design

We design and set up the control architecture for your agentic or zero-human company — translating the principles of meaningful human control into concrete, operational safeguards. From guardrails that prevent agents from taking harmful actions, to human-in-the-loop checkpoints for high-stakes decisions, to emergency brake mechanisms that can halt operations instantly.

  • Guardrails — hard limits on what agents can and cannot do autonomously
  • Human-in-the-loop — approval gates for irreversible or high-cost decisions
  • Human-on-the-loop — monitoring and override capability without blocking flow
  • Emergency brake — one-action kill switch to pause all agent operations
  • Governance policy document and implementation guidance included

Payments

Escrow Setup for On-Chain Payments

We set up smart-contract-based escrow infrastructure for your on-chain payment flows. Whether settling between agents, clients, or autonomous organisations, escrow ensures funds are held securely and released only when agreed conditions are met — with no single party in control.

  • Smart-contract escrow deployed on Base or Ethereum mainnet
  • Configurable release conditions — milestone, time-lock, or multi-party approval
  • Safe multisig custody for locked funds
  • Agent-triggered or human-approved release flows
  • Supports ETH, USDC, and other ERC-20 tokens
  • Full audit trail and on-chain verification
