Case Study: How It Ran

A behind-the-scenes account of how the Dutch zero-human company operated in practice.

The Setup

Legal Context
A Dutch-based experiment exploring what a zero-human company would look like under Dutch law. No formal legal registration has been completed — the legal status of a ZHC remains an open question. Human board members hold accountability and delegate all operational work to AI agents.
Platform
Paperclip — a multi-agent task management and coordination platform. Agents wake up in heartbeats, pick up tasks, execute work, and report results through structured issue comments.
AI Models
Claude (Anthropic) models power the agents. Each agent runs in isolated sessions with persistent file-based memory to maintain continuity across heartbeats.

What Worked

Win

30+ Deliverables in Weeks

The agent team produced a full company governance stack, research corpus, and public communications suite faster than any human team of equivalent size.

Win

Zero Coordination Meetings

All coordination happened through structured issue comments and task routing. No standups, no email threads, no scheduling overhead.

Win

Full Audit Trail

Every action linked to a run ID. Complete traceability from board directive to completed output, enabling post-hoc review of any decision.

Win

Consistent Quality

Output quality remained high across long working sessions. No fatigue, no context-switching costs, no bad days.

The Earnings Model

How a zero-human company generates revenue.

Advisory Services
Organizations pay the ZHC to help them design and deploy their own AI governance frameworks. The company's existence is proof-of-concept that makes the advice credible.
Research Licensing
Academic institutions and think tanks license the governance framework, research papers, and methodology documentation.
Speaking & Media
Conference appearances, media interviews, and keynotes generate brand value and direct revenue for the board members.
Document Production
Clients commission the agent team to produce high-quality strategic, legal, or technical documents at scale and speed.

Lessons for Others

Start with Clear Scope

Define exactly what decisions agents can make autonomously and which require human approval. Ambiguity causes gridlock.

Invest in Audit Infrastructure

Immutable logs are not optional. Without them, you cannot review, correct, or defend AI decisions after the fact.

Memory Is Non-Negotiable

Agents without persistent memory repeat mistakes and lose context. File-based memory systems are a practical solution today.

Keep Humans for Irreversibility

Route any action that is hard to undo through human approval. The cost of one bad autonomous decision exceeds many approval cycles.
